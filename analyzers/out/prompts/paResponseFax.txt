The document above is a **paResponseFax**.

Payer prior-authorization response sheets and portal determination printouts — determinations laid out as a form rather than a letter. Extracts patient, payer and case identity, the outcome and any pending status, the approved period when granted, and — the field that matters most on a pending response — the itemised list of information the payer is still waiting for.

Extract the fields listed below from that document, into the required JSON object.

## Rules

1. **Read values off the page. Do not calculate, infer or complete them.** If a field asks for a total and no total is printed, the value is null even when you could add the numbers yourself.
2. **A field that is not in the document gets `value: null`.** Never guess, never carry a value over from a similar field, and never substitute a plausible default. An honest null is worth more than a likely-looking value, because a null is routed to review and a wrong value is not.
3. **Copy text exactly as printed** — same spelling, same punctuation, same capitalisation, including anything that looks like an OCR error. Do not tidy, expand abbreviations, or reformat dates. Normalisation happens downstream and needs the original.
4. **Where the same fact appears more than once, prefer the copy in the field's own labelled block.** Header and footer repeats are often stale.
5. **`quote` must be a verbatim substring of the document above** — copy it, do not retype it from memory. It is checked automatically against the document text, and a quote that is not found there marks the value as ungrounded. Keep it short: the few words around the value are enough. Set it to null when `value` is null.
6. **`confidence` must reflect what you actually saw.** Above 0.9 means you read it clearly and there was no other candidate. Below 0.7 means the text was unclear, or you had to choose. A field you are unsure about with an honest 0.5 is useful; the same field at 0.95 is actively harmful, because it will skip human review.

## Fields

### PatientName
*string · transcription*

Patient first and last name, usually at the top of the page or in a demographics block. May be labeled Patient, Patient Name, Member, or appear as Last, First. On an insurance card this is the member name.

### PatientDOB
*date · transcription*

Patient date of birth. Labeled DOB, D.O.B., Date of Birth, or Birth Date. Typically MM/DD/YYYY. Do not confuse with the date the form was written, the date of service, or a coverage effective date.

Copy the date exactly as printed. Do not convert the format.

### MemberID
*string · transcription*

Insurance member or subscriber identifier. Labeled Member ID, Member #, Subscriber ID, Policy ID, Identification Number, or ID. Copy the alphanumerics exactly as printed, including any leading letters.

### PayerName
*string · resolution*

Insurance company, health plan, or pharmacy benefit manager name that issued or administers this document. Usually in the letterhead or the card face — for example Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas, Express Scripts, CVS Caremark, OptumRx.

### CaseNumber
*string · transcription*

Payer case, reference or tracking number for this request. Labeled Case #, Reference #, Ref #, Request ID, or Determination ID. This identifies the REQUEST; AuthorizationNumber identifies a granted authorization.

### AuthorizationNumber
*string · transcription*

Authorization or certification number granted by the payer. Labeled Auth #, Authorization Number, Certification Number, or PA Number. Return null when nothing was authorized, even if a case number is present.

### PAOutcome
*string · resolution*

The determination this document communicates. Read the decision sentence or the status field in the body, never the letterhead or the subject line. Partially approved covers an approval granted at a reduced quantity, duration or strength. Pending covers a request still in review or held for more information.

Allowed values: `Approved`, `Denied`, `Partially approved`, `Pending`, `Cancelled`, `Not stated`. Use null if none of them apply.

### ProviderName
*string · transcription*

Prescribing or treating provider name, sometimes with a credential such as MD, DO, NP, or PA. Labeled Provider, Prescriber, Physician, Ordering Provider, or Doctor. Not the practice or facility name.

### ProviderNPI
*string · transcription*

Prescribing provider National Provider Identifier. Always exactly 10 digits. Labeled NPI, NPI #, Provider NPI, or NPI Number. If more than one NPI appears, take the one belonging to the prescribing or ordering provider, not the facility, group or dispensing pharmacy.

### ProviderFax
*string · transcription*

Fax number for the provider office, as printed. Labeled Fax, Fax #, or Fax Number. On payer correspondence this is the fax the response is to be returned to; take the provider-side number when both appear.

### DrugName
*string · resolution*

Drug or therapy name as written, brand or generic, including any strength printed alongside it — for example "Humira (adalimumab) 40 mg/0.8 mL" or "Stelara 90 mg". Copy what is on the page; reference-data resolution happens downstream.

### ResponseDate
*date · transcription*

Date the payer issued this response, from the form's date field. Not the fax transmission timestamp in the page banner.

Copy the date exactly as printed. Do not convert the format.

### RequestReceivedDate
*date · transcription*

Date the payer records having received the original request. Labeled Received, Date Received, or Request Date.

Copy the date exactly as printed. Do not convert the format.

### FormOrPortalName
*string · transcription*

Title of the response form or the portal it was printed from, as it appears at the top of the page — for example "Prior Authorization Determination Notice" or "CoverMyMeds Request Summary".

### ApprovedThroughDate
*date · transcription*

Last date of the authorised period, where the response grants one. Null unless the outcome is an approval.

Copy the date exactly as printed. Do not convert the format.

### ApprovedQuantity
*string · transcription*

Quantity or days supply authorised, as printed, including the period it applies over.

### PendingReason
*string · resolution*

Why the request is not yet decided. Not applicable when the response reports a final outcome. Not documented when it reports pending without saying why.

Allowed values: `Additional clinical information required`, `Under clinical review`, `Awaiting peer-to-peer`, `Member eligibility verification`, `Referred to medical director`, `Not applicable`, `Not documented`. Use null if none of them apply.

### InformationRequested
*array · inference*

Exactly what the payer is asking for before it will decide — chart notes for a date range, a specific lab result, a step-therapy attestation, a corrected NDC. One entry per item, worded as the form words it. This list is what a follow-up to the prescriber's office is built from, so keep each item specific enough to act on and do not merge two requests into one entry. Empty array when nothing further is requested.

### ResponseDueDate
*date · transcription*

Date by which the requested information must be returned before the request is closed or denied. Labeled Response Due, Reply By, or Information Due.

Copy the date exactly as printed. Do not convert the format.

### DenialReasonCode
*string · transcription*

Reason code printed beside a denied outcome, exactly as it appears. Null when the outcome is not a denial or no code is given.

### ContinuationOfTherapy
*string · inference*

Whether this authorises continuation of therapy the patient is already receiving rather than a new start. Infer from renewal, reauthorization, or continued-use language on the form.

Allowed values: `Yes`, `No`, `Not documented`. Use null if none of them apply.

### ReviewerName
*string · transcription*

Name of the reviewer, nurse or coordinator who completed the response, with any credential printed beside it.

### CallbackPhone
*string · transcription*

Telephone number the form gives for questions about this response.

### ReturnFaxNumber
*string · transcription*

Fax number the completed sheet or the requested information is to be returned to, usually beside a "fax this back to" instruction.
